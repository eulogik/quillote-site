Policy Privacy

Your files are yours.

Quillote does not want your data. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no sync service. This page is short because the policy is short.

Last updated 27 October 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Quillote has no account system, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no advertising. The app reads and writes the files you open and keeps its own preferences and crash snapshots on your device. It does not send them anywhere.

Network requests

The app makes a network request only when you choose to open a link in your browser. There is no update check in the public beta. A future update mechanism will be opt-in or clearly disclosed before it ships.

This website

This site is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics, and makes no requests to third-party domains. Fonts are served from this domain. The only external links are to Eulogik and GitHub, and they open only when you click them.

Email

If you email hello@quillote.com, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and nothing else. Ask us to delete the thread and we will.

Files on your phone

The phone app copies a file you pick into its own sandbox so it can edit it. The original stays where it was. A new file saves in the same sandbox folder. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no sync service. Share-sheet export is not in 0.1.3 yet; until it ships, saved copies reopen from Recents inside Quillote. Uninstalling the app removes its sandbox, including those copies.

Children

Quillote is a general-purpose writing tool. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A change that adds collection would be announced before it takes effect, not buried in a checkbox.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@quillote.com. Quillote is a product of Eulogik.
